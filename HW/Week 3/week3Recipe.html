Ham Sandwhich Recipe

A simple ham sandwich made with savory ham, fresh vegetables, and your favorite condiments. It’s quick, easy, and perfect for lunch or a light meal.

Recipe Information

Prep Time: 15 minutes

Cook Time: 30 minutes

Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 2 slices of bread
  • 3–4 slices of ham
  • 1 slice of cheese
  • Lettuce
  • 2–3 tomato slices
  • Mayonnaise or mustard

Instructions

  1. Lay the two slices of bread on a plate.
  2. Spread mayonnaise or mustard on one side of each slice.
  3. Add the ham, cheese, lettuce, and tomato.
  4. Place the second slice of bread on top.
  5. Cut the sandwich in half and serve.
